About Handpoke

What Are Handpoke Tattoos?

  • Handpoke tattoos are created using only my hand, needle, and ink.
  • Each dot is carefully placed on your skin, gradually building delicate lines.
  • The end result is a one of a kind tattoo made just for you.
  • The handpoked experience has been described as less painful but sensitivity varies by person.
  • This style has a vintage, pencil drawn texture like illustrations from old story books.
