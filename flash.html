Flash or Custom?

What is a “Flash” Tattoo?

All designs are pre drawn, are original works created by the artist (moi) and can be tattooed more than once. You see it, you like it, I tattoo it. Flash also has a preset price range so you know what you’ll pay for the tattoo. Flash is perfect if you want something small or spontaneous.

What makes a custom tattoo different?

A custom tattoo involves you explaining your vision, I draw something, we work together to refine it and then I tattoo the final product. For custom pieces, the price is hourly instead of a set price.

Pricing for custom pieces:

There is not deposit required to book an appointment.

  • First hour is $100 and every hour after is $50+
  • 1 hour = $100
  • 2 hours = $150
